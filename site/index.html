Below the surface.
Three deep characters.

Ocean 0.24 goes deeper. Fathom, Undertow and Spume are a family of three characters built on one dense 16-line network, and they bring the count to eight: a slow tide, three reversed voices locked to the host tempo, and an attack dissolved into spray. Current, the coherent flow field, still carries the surface. In the editor the character now sits in a centred list with step arrows on either side and its description beside the Evolution knob, and Freeze keeps new sound out in every character.

Harmony

A halo drawn toward
the notes already present.

Harmonic Gravity listens to the dry signal, builds a confidence-weighted pitch-class map and gently excites stable resonant modes after the FDN. It does not retune the existing tail or force the signal into a guessed key.

  1. 01
    ListenC2–B6 pitch-class analysis
  2. 02
    TrustReject transient and overtone errors
  3. 03
    Glow36 stable stereo modes, C3–B5

At 0%, Harmony is an exact bypass. Uncertain or monophonic material stays deliberately subtle; Freeze holds the current map.

Under the surface

Energy-stable by design.
Alive by intention.

Ocean begins with an eight-line Feedback Delay Network. Prime-scaled, decorrelated lengths feed an orthonormal matrix, so density can grow without the loop inventing energy. Character processing changes how the space develops—not whether it remains stable. Fathom, Undertow and Spume run beside it on a denser 16-line network of their own and trade places with the core in a 200 ms crossfade.
