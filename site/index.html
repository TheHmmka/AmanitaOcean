Harmony

A halo drawn toward
the notes already present.

Harmonic Gravity listens to the dry signal, builds a confidence-weighted pitch-class map and gently excites stable resonant modes after the FDN. It does not retune the existing tail or force the signal into a guessed key.

  1. 01
    ListenC2–B6 pitch-class analysis
  2. 02
    TrustReject transient and overtone errors
  3. 03
    Glow36 stable stereo modes, C3–B5

At 0%, Harmony is an exact bypass. Uncertain or monophonic material stays deliberately subtle; Freeze holds the current map.

Focus

Clarity without making
the ocean collapse.

Focus is perceptual masking relief, not conventional full-band ducking. It finds only the wet frequency regions that obscure an active dry signal and opens temporary, cut-only pockets.

Under the surface

Energy-stable by design.
Alive by intention.

Ocean begins with an eight-line Feedback Delay Network. Prime-scaled, decorrelated lengths feed an orthonormal matrix, so density can grow without the loop inventing energy. Character processing changes how the space develops—not whether it remains stable.
