# converters_verification: the packet `converters`, checked by an independent skeptic

Wave 2, 7 October 2026. Checked: `findings/converters.md`, `converters.py`,
`fit_converters.py`, `tide_structural_data/converters.json`.

Tags: MEASURED (read off captures), FITTED (residual given), INFERRED, OPEN. A
null is `20 log10(rms(candidate - reference) / rms(reference))` with no gain,
delay or polarity fitted. Everything is Macro 0, Mix 100 %, neutral baseline,
10 s warm-up, no input sample above 0.5 (also not in the converted 44.1 kHz
stimuli), unless stated.

## 0. Verdict

The packet's core is right and is now MEASURED at 47 host rates the author did
not capture. A second implementation of the law, written from the text of
`findings/converters.md` and not from `converters.py`, reproduces the reference
at the same rounding floor with fresh stimuli, other settings, other warm-ups
and other host block sizes:

- **Kernel, gains, latency rule, both delay formulas, the one-or-two-block
  rule: CONFIRMED everywhere**, -126.2 to -128.9 dB on my 2 s stimulus,
  -131.6 to -132.1 dB on the campaign's programme with another seed.
- **The clock rule is right at the five product rates** (nine sign pairs tried
  at each; the stated pair wins by 48 to 56 dB). `converters.clock_signs`
  predicted 64 of 65 visible drifting signs at new rates.
- **Three statements do not hold as written:**
  1. `converters.clock_signs` is wrong at 56 kHz (output clock: predicted high,
     MEASURED low). `converters.py` nulls there at -76.7 dB instead of
     -128.6 dB, and at -74.6 instead of -79.7 dB behind the network model.
  2. "Nothing drifts" is not true. The clock error is real and grows linearly
     with the time since the start. At 383999 Hz it is read off the captures
     (7.37 to 7.40e-10 internal sample per second, the simulation says 7.40e-10)
     and the sign-only law of `converters.py` nulls at -117.5 dB after 10 s and
     -110.4 dB after 40 s. At 56 kHz the output sign alternates inside every
     block for at least the first 5.3 s. At the product rates neither effect
     shows (48 kHz up to 160 s, 192 kHz up to 90 s after the start).
  3. "Nothing in the wet path besides the converters depends on the host rate"
     holds for Decay, Size, Width, Brightness, Return and Master. It fails with
     the input filter or Ducking moved (-21 to -48 dB), which are neutral in
     the campaign's target.
- Macro above 0 stays OPEN. A statistical test at Macro 100 % finds no
  difference between rates but resolves only about -15 dB.

- Clock signs were taken from the table of the findings at the measured rates

- About 0.8 GB of captures.

### 3.5 The clock behind the signs

**Prediction at new rates.** MEASURED. Of my 47 rates the output sign is

**56 kHz, the exception.** MEASURED, `v08_drift.py`, `v08d_sign_in_time.py`.
`clock_signs(56000)` gives (low, high); the captures say (low, low). The
author's own `fit_converters.relation` on his probe gives -76.40 dB at 56 kHz

In the early capture no constant sign fits. Sample by sample (every output
sample on an exact entry, 24639 with a verdict clearer than 20 dB): 15968 show
"low", 8255 "high", 416 "exact", in 906 runs of mostly 5 to 45 samples
(`v08e_runs.py`): the clock error passes through exactly zero again and again,
block after block (a block is 56 samples). From 10 s on all 24621 samples show
"low"; at 96 kHz, 3 to 5.3 s after the start, all 42149 show "high". In the
simulation the output clocks at 56, 64 and 96 kHz
are the slowest (1.4e-12 to 4.3e-12 sample per second; up to 7.5e-10

**The error has a size.** MEASURED, `v08_drift.py`, `v08b_staircase.py`. At
383999 Hz (L = 54857) the fractional table positions lie 1/54857 entry apart.
A clock that is low by `e` internal samples lowers every tap within `4096 e`
above an entry, so the number of lowered positions measures `e` in units of
`1 / (4096 * 54857)` = 4.4505e-9 internal sample.

per second**, low, linear from the start of processing.

`converters.Clock` with blocks of 384 host samples gives 7.400e-10 per second
(7 units at 42.102 s). That is 0.01 % above the bracket, whose width is 0.34 %:
4 ms early against the last sample that still shows fewer than 7 units (2 ms
if the two blocks of output delay are allowed for). This is the strongest
evidence for the author's mechanism: not a sign but a rate, to three digits.
It also bounds the bookkeeping: one sample more look-ahead in the output clock
moves the crossing to 40.43 s, blocks of 385 to 42.07 s, 383 to 42.15 s
(inside), 380 to 42.20 s (inside), 376 to 42.27 s (outside). A variant in
which the output converter makes exactly `latency` host samples per block and

the miss again 56 kHz (`v08c_blockfromdrift.py`, `v08f_pull.py`). So the block
is 380 to 384 host samples, and the exact bookkeeping is still OPEN.

**What follows.** The "sign" is what the clock error does while it is smaller
than the spacing of fractional table positions, `gcd(L, 4096) / (4096 L)`
internal sample. INFERRED from the simulated drifts: at 48, 96 and 192 kHz
that spacing is 4.9e-5 sample and is reached after 28 hours at the earliest
(192 kHz output clock, 4.9e-10 per second). Within any session the five pairs
of section 3.4 are constants.

## 4. Weak spots a product should know about

1. **Use the measured sign pairs, not `clock_signs`, and only where a null is
   wanted.** 48 and 96 kHz: input low, output high. 192 kHz: input high,
   output low. 88.2 and 176.4 kHz: exact. A wrong sign costs -65 to -80 dB
   relative to the wet signal and nothing audible. At 56 kHz the module is
   wrong.
2. **Validate converters with the exact relation, never with the network
   score.** A wrong description can sit at -89 dB (96 kHz: delays moved by one
   lattice step each way, signs reversed) and score like the right one behind a
   model whose floor is -80 dB.
3. **The start.** The reference is not repeatable in its first 1.5 s or more,
   and the state of the converters in their first blocks is unverified. At a
   rate with a nearly balanced clock (56 kHz) the lookup pattern needs 5 to
   10 s to settle. A product should start its converters from silence and not
   try to null the first seconds.
4. **44.1 kHz is a special case with a jump.** No converters and 88 samples
   (1.9955 ms) at 44100 Hz; 188.0016 samples (4.263 ms) at 44101 Hz. Against
   the dry signal the wet is 0.998 ms late at 44.1 kHz and 3.07 to 3.24 ms late
   at 48, 96 and 192 kHz, 2.08 to 2.14 ms at 88.2 and 176.4 kHz.
5. **Macro above 0**, the owner's listening point, is supported only by the
   statistics of section 3.8.
6. **Other controls.** The one-core-between-two-converters picture is verified
   for Decay, Size, Width, Brightness, Return, Master. It is not valid for the
   reference's input filter and ducking.
7. **DC.** Through both converters a constant comes back 1.0004 to 1.0010 times
   as large (+0.003 to +0.009 dB), depending on rate and phase (computed from
   the law at the five product rates; the author gives 1.0006 to 1.0008).
8. **Rates.** Nothing below 44100 Hz or above 384000 Hz can be rendered. The
   rules for 32 kHz and 768 kHz are extrapolations.
