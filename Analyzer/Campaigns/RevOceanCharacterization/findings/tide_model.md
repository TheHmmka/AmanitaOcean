# tide_model: the complete model of the reference in Tide mode, Macro 0 to 100 %

Packet `tide_model`, wave 2, 7 October 2026. Model: `tide_model.py`. Scorer: `score_tide.py`.
Evidence and scores: `tide_structural_data/tide_model.json`, regenerated by `fit_tide_model.py`
(about 20 minutes on four cores with the captures cached; the sections it was
run twice for came out identical). Reference: Arturia Rev OCEAN
1.0.0.5848, Tide mode, neutral baseline.

Tags: MEASURED (read off captures), FITTED (a constant, residual given), INFERRED (reasoned, not
tested), OPEN. A null is `20 log10(rms(model - reference) / rms(reference))` with no gain, delay or
polarity fitted.

## 0. Operating point

- **Mix 100 % in every capture.** Macro (the Tide amount) 100 % is the main point; 75, 50, 25, 10,
  5 and 2 % give the range. Neutral baseline otherwise (Brightness 0 %, Width 100 %, input filter
  open, no pre-delay), every input sample at or below 0.5.
- Hosts at 44.1, 48, 88.2 and 96 kHz; Decay 0.5 to 11 s, Size 62 to 181 %; warm-ups of 1, 6.1,
  10, 12.5 and 30 s; three kinds of stimulus (a 14 s programme, impulse trains, steady noise).
- Captures of this packet: 0.27 GB of cache (the guideline is 1 GB). No locked holdout is read
  by any file of the packet; the model's constants come from the other packets, and the one
  law fitted here (the rate) is fitted on captures no score uses.

## 1. Result

```
host in -> converter -> equaliser -> [ cos(90° m) + sin(90° m) comb ] -> 44 samples -> 2 x 16 lines, Hadamard loop
        -> per output:  lines 1 to 8 -> voice at phase phi,  lines 9 to 16 -> voice at phi + 1/2  -> sum -> converter -> host out
phi_o(t) = level_o(t) + rate(m) t      one phase per output, t from the first processed sample
```

`render(stimulus, sample_rate, warmup_seconds, decay_seconds, size_percent, macro_percent, seed, phase=None)`
joins `converters.py`, `network_model.py`, `tide_stage.py` (comb, voice) and `tide_phase.py` (the
level of the phase). Three things are this packet's own:

1. **The split between the voices** (section 2). The output taps of lines 1 to 8 of a group pass
   the voice at the phase of that group's output, those of lines 9 to 16 the same voice law half
   a cycle away, each line with its Macro 0 tap weight. The cross-fed part of a line goes with
   the line. MEASURED.
2. **The equaliser sits in front of the comb**, not behind it (section 6). MEASURED.
3. **The rate law of the phase** (section 5): `rate(m) = 0.0431885 + 0.0070490 e^(0.63 m)`
   cycles/s, 0.0502375 at Macro 0 and 0.0564238 at Macro 100 %, within 3.5e-7 of the readings at
   seven Macro values. FITTED. The law of `tide_phase.py` is up to 5.4e-4 away at low Macro.

Where the model stands:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Exact** (bit for bit) | Macro 0: `render` returns `network_model.render`. The general path with Macro set to 0 differs from it by -309.9 dB |
| **Exact up to the phase** | Macro above 0: with the phase of each output described by 5 to 11 numbers per 14 s, the whole response nulls at **-88.8 to -106.8 dB at Macro 100 %** (8 cases, mean -98.5 dB), -91.6 to -105.7 dB at 50 %, -96.1 to -100.8 dB at 25 % (section 4). At Macro 2, 5 and 10 % a model-free phase gives -101.9 to -107.2 dB (section 5) |
| **Statistical** | the phase itself: start level, knot times and knot values of each output are random per instance. With its own generator the model is another draw of the reference: every impulse descriptor of `targets.json` at 0.15 to 0.44 of the reference's spread at Macro 100 % (the reference against itself: 0.28 to 0.48), and the level cycle under noise within 1.8 standard errors on every number (sections 7 and 8) |
| **Missing** | the first 3 s of a fresh instance, a Macro that moves, the joint law of the two start levels, everything outside the neutral baseline (section 10) |

## 3. The model

Everything between the converters runs at 44.1 kHz. `m` is Macro as a fraction.

```
first, internal   = network_model.network_input(stimulus, sample_rate, warmup)        input converter
driven            = tide_input(network_model.equalise(internal), first, m)            equaliser, then per input
                                                                                      cos(90° m) x + sin(90° m) comb(x)
taps[.][o][v]     = line_taps(driven, first + 44, decay, size)                        network; v = 0: lines 1-8, v = 1: lines 9-16,
                                                                                      each line times its Macro 0 tap weight
internal out[.][o]= voice(taps[.][o][0], phi_o) + voice(taps[.][o][1], phi_o + 1/2)   tide_stage's state-variable low-pass and gain
host              = converters.to_host(internal out, ...)                             output converter (44 samples at 44.1 kHz)
```

- The comb counts its delay from the first processed sample; the cross feed of the network comes
  behind it (`tide_stage`), so each comb belongs to an input and each phase to an output.
- A voice sets cut-off, Q and gain once per 44 samples, at read counts that are multiples of 44,
  from the phase at that sample; Q follows its target through the one-pole of `tide_stage`
  (time constant 10 blocks), started 150 blocks early.
- `phase=None` runs the generator: `tide_phase.TidePhase(seed).levels(t) + phase_rate(m) t`.
  `phase=f` uses `f(seconds) -> (2, n)` instead; the scorer nulls a capture with it.
- Macro 0 returns `network_model.render` itself. The general path at Macro 0 (comb weight 0,
  voice at 20 kHz, Q 1, gain 1) agrees with it at -309.9 dB (MEASURED, 14 s programme at 48 kHz).
- A render of 14 s takes 0.25 s at a 44.1 kHz host, 0.6 s at 48 kHz and 1.1 s at 96 kHz.

## 5. The phase at the precision of a null

**The hold is a straight line.** Fourteen captures of the programme that start 1 s after the
instance (44.1 kHz, Decay 2 s, Macro 100, 75, 50, 25, 10, 5, 2 %, two realisations each; `rate`
in the data file). A spline with knots 0.25 s apart, with no idea of a hold, nulls them at
**-101.9 to -109.5 dB** (so the model holds from Macro 2 to 100 %, including the blend below
Macro 5.68 %). From 3 s after the start to 0.5 s before the first knot of its output that
spline is a straight line to 8e-8 to 3.4e-6 cycle rms in 24 of 28 outputs (6.8e-6 and 1.1e-5
in two more). MEASURED. `tide_phase.md` had the hold straight to 7e-4 cycle. The two right
outputs at Macro 2 % read 1.2e-4 and 1.7e-4: there the phase passes 0.5, where the gain window
has its maximum and the voice hardly depends on the phase; they are left out of the law.

**Rate law.** FITTED. Slope of those straight lines, mean of four outputs per Macro value (two
at Macro 2 %), range of the readings 7e-8 to 1e-6:

| Macro | 2 % | 5 % | 10 % | 25 % | 50 % | 75 % | 100 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rate, cycles/s | 0.0503272 | 0.0504631 | 0.0506959 | 0.0514399 | 0.0528473 | 0.0544952 | 0.0564239 |
| model, `0.0431885 + 0.0070490 e^(0.63 m)`, minus measured | -3.5e-7 | -3e-8 | -1e-8 | 0 | +1.9e-7 | -9e-8 | -6e-8 |
| law of `tide_phase.py` minus measured | +5.4e-4 | +5.1e-4 | +4.5e-4 | +2.9e-4 | +8.6e-5 | -2.2e-5 | +2.6e-5 |

- A free fit of `A + B e^(k m)` gives A = 0.0431936, B = 0.0070441, k = 0.63032 with residuals
  up to 1.9e-7; with the round k = 0.63 (A = 0.0431887, B = 0.0070489) they are up to 2.7e-7,
  the scatter of the readings. The model uses k = 0.63 as the vendor's Exp(0.63) map from
  0.0502375 (Macro 0) to 0.0564238 cycles/s (Macro 100 %), periods of 19.905 and 17.723 s.
- Tried and rejected at this precision (exploration): k = 0.88 x 0.71337 (the amount of the
  cut-off law times the shape of the Size law; residuals up to 1.0e-6), k = 0.625 (2.2e-6), a
  parabola (1.5e-5).
  No round form found for the two end values, in Hz, in seconds or per 44-sample block. OPEN.
- Without a hold the generator-form fits of section 4 give 0.0564223 +- 0.0000095 (16 outputs,
  hosts at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz), 0.0528518 +- 0.000011 and 0.0514469 +- 0.000017 at Macro 100, 50
  and 25 %: the same law at every host rate, less sharply.
- The earlier value 0.0564 +- 0.0002 (`tide_phase_verification.md`) is confirmed and narrowed
  several hundred times; 0.05645 and 0.0565 are excluded.

**The raised cosines.** MEASURED. In the fifteen cases the generator form and the spline with
knots 0.25 s apart, fitted independently, agree to 9e-7 to 2.6e-5 cycle rms in 26 of 30
outputs (4.8e-5 to 1.9e-4 in the others, where the spline is the worse of the two). All 67
knots that fall inside a capture lie in a cell of their own (cells of 7.353 s on the left and
6.25 s on the right, counted from the first processed sample); knot values lie between 0.2008
and 0.5699 (left) and 0.0207 and 0.4238 (right), start levels of the fourteen hold captures
between 0.217 and 0.527 and between 0.074 and 0.436. So grid, hold and shape of
`tide_phase.py` hold at 1e-5 cycle, a hundred times closer than that packet could read them.
One start level on the right (0.4362) lies 0.003 above its upper bound of 0.433, inside the
+-0.004 that packet gives.

**The first seconds.** MEASURED. Extended back to 1.5 s after the start, the straight line of
the hold misses the spline by -2.3e-5 to -7.4e-5 cycle at Macro 100 %, -1.6e-5 to -2.3e-5 at
75 %, -3e-6 to -1.0e-5 at 50 %, +5e-6 to +4.1e-5 at 25 % and +6e-6 to +9.4e-5 at 10 %. The sign
turns near Macro 50 %. The generator form, which has no such start, nulls these captures at
-80 dB (Macro 100 and 10 %), -91 to -95 dB (75 %), -86 to -93 dB (25 %) and -104 dB (50 %),
while the free spline stays at -103 to -109 dB. (In one half second of a Macro 100 % capture
the two descriptions differ by 7e-6 cycle and null at -107 and -85 dB; exploration `s09`. That
is the sensitivity quoted in section 4.) INFERRED: the instance starts from a Macro near
50 % and settles more slowly than the 0.6 s of `tide_phase_verification.md`; it is over by 3 s.
The model does not have it.

## 9. What failed on the way

- A running sum for the fit weights buried the tail under rounding and left it unfitted
  (-1 dB after 8 s); block sums and a logarithmic interpolation follow the response over 600 dB.
- Fitting below the reference's output floor (1e-36, `findings/network.md`) at Decay 0.65 s sent
  the spline to hundreds of cycles; samples whose local rms is below 1e-30 are left out.
- Knots 0.5 s apart, the description the task proposed, are too coarse: one case stops at
  -38.8 dB where the generator form reaches -101.7 dB.
- The first generator-form fits let the rate go free from the start and one train ended with a
  negative rate (-32 dB); the search now starts at the model's rate and keeps within 2 % of it
  until the audio fit.
- At Macro 2 % the right output's phase is not determined where it passes 0.5 (two readings
  left out of the rate law).
- The model first had the comb in front of the equaliser, as `tide_stage` has it; the ablation
  that was meant to show the order does not matter showed the opposite order to be right.

## 10. What is still missing for a listener at Macro 100 %, Mix 100 %

Nothing that a null at -89 to -107 dB or the descriptors of `targets.json` can show at the
neutral baseline, once an instance is 3 s old. What the model does not have:

1. **The start of an instance.** For up to 3 s the reference behaves as if Macro came from about
   50 % (section 5; -80 dB at Macro 100 % for a response that starts at 1.25 s). Not modelled.
2. **A Macro that moves.** Never measured by anyone; the start shows that it is smoothed.
3. **The joint law of the two start levels** and whatever seeds them (`tide_phase` and its
   verification: tied, batch dependent). The model draws them as `tide_phase.py` does.
   Bounds of the level are known to +-0.004 cycle.
4. **Everything outside the neutral baseline**: Brightness, Width, pre-delay, the input filter,
   Mix below 100 %, input peaks above 0.5 (the level-dependent stage of `findings/io.md`), the
   output clipper, Freeze. The shell of `findings/io.md` is not in `render`.
5. **The comb's delay arithmetic.** Single precision, not identified; INFERRED to be what keeps
   impulse trains near -89 to -96 dB.
6. **Hosts below 44.1 kHz**: not capturable.

Not established here: why the rate law has these end values; the level cycle at Macro values
other than 100 % (only the statistics of section 7 cover Macro 50 %); free-running statistics
at Macro values other than 100 and 50 % (the targets hold no others); what limits the null of
impulse trains.
